Why collectors stop at Thousand Sunny

The shop is named for the ship. The greeter at the door is Gear 5. The case already holds Luffy, Shanks, Nami, Ace, and Hancock slabs. This is a collector shop that treats One Piece TCG as a real line, not a side rack next to sports wax.

Questions before you come aboard

Do you carry One Piece TCG sealed and singles?

Yes — booster boxes, packs, starter decks, and singles when we have them. Ask the crew what’s on the wall this week.

Yes. Binders, singles, and sealed. Start an offer from the site or walk in.
