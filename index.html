Why the Sunny

More than
the next pull.

Thousand Sunny is built around the part of collecting that happens after the pack opens: comparing a new hit, finding the missing piece, meeting another player, or settling in for one more round.

The shelves hold cards, anime, figures, manga and snacks. The tables make room for the people behind them. Whether you arrive with a grail in mind or no plan at all, the goal is the same: leave with a better story than the one you walked in with.

Before you come

Good questions.

A few quick answers for collectors, players and first-time visitors.

Do you buy cards and collections?

Yes. Bring a single, a binder or a larger collection. The crew can look at cards and collectibles and talk through cash or store-credit options.

Can I trade instead of sell?

Absolutely. Use the offer starter above to tell the crew what you have and what you are hunting, then bring the conversation into the shop.

Do you help with grading and consignment?

Yes. The display-case team can talk through grading help and consignment terms in person, including what makes sense for your item.

Do I need to book a table or room?

For a specific table, room or group plan, message the crew first so they can check what is open before you come over.

How do I find the crew at a weekend card show?

Check the show board above. If a venue is not posted yet, send a quick text and the crew can point you in the right direction.
