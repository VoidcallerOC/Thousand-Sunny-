What we do

Four ways to
come aboard

Whether you're hunting a grail slab, cashing in a collection, or just here to nerd out over your favorite anime — the gangplank's down and the crew's friendly. This is a shop built by collectors, for collectors.

01

Buy

Singles, sealed product, figures, Funko Pops, manga and Japanese snacks — new arrivals landing every week.

02

Sell

Turn cards and collectibles into cash or store credit. Bring in a single, a binder or a whole collection for a fair offer.

03

Trade

Level up your deck and your shelf. Trade with the shop and the community to track down exactly what you're after.

04

Collect

Graded slabs, chase figures and statues, grails behind the glass — plus consignment and grading help.

Pull up a chair

Play & gather

The Sunny isn't just a shop — it's a hangout. Big communal tables, private rooms and screens for game nights, tournaments, releases and just chilling with the crew. Come throw down or come hang out.

Event schedule rotates — follow us on Instagram @tscc_ct for game nights, tournaments and release parties.

Behind the glass

The display case

Our showcase is stocked with graded slabs, vintage grails and chase pieces — think PSA 10 Pokémon, One Piece hits and hard-to-find figures. Looking to buy a grail, consign your collection, or get cards sent off for grading? Come talk to the crew.
